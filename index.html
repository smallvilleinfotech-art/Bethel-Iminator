About us

A habit that drained them. A platform that builds them.

Betting is now a phone tap away. For some young people it starts as entertainment and becomes a habit that drains money, strains families and dims ambition. The problem is not a lack of ambition.

“Bethel Iminators” is a coded play on “bet eliminators”. We are building a community that helps people leave harmful betting behind and find what to start doing instead. We are not building another betting platform.

Become a Bethel Iminators supporter

A little from many. A better future for someone. Give ₦100 a month or ₦1,200 once a year to help sustain the programmes that move young people beyond harmful betting.

How it works

  • Choose monthly (₦100) or yearly (₦1,200) and pay by debit card through Paystack. It renews automatically on the same date.

Real resources depend on how many people join, payment costs and running costs, and we will report what comes in and how it is used.

Recurring payments need a debit card. Bank transfer and USSD cannot renew automatically.

Founder

Meet our founder

Our founder is a young data analyst and youth mentor who has personally navigated the terrain of gambling, often dressed up as “speculative trading” and “wagering”. He serves as a programme officer with the Boys to Kings Mentoring Programme and is the convener of the National Assembly of Bible Scholars and The 36 Innovative Delegates.

Over the years he has quietly studied and recorded patterns across many anonymised cases of problem gambling among young people. Bethel Iminators is his answer to what he found.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people ask us

What is Bethel Iminators?

Bethel Iminators is a community and mentorship initiative, a product of The 36 Innovative Delegates, that helps young Nigerians move away from harmful betting and problem gambling towards recovery support, financial wellbeing, skills and enterprise. The name is a coded play on “bet eliminators”.

Who is Bethel Iminators for?

Boys and young men and women who want to leave harmful betting behind, parents and guardians who are worried about them, and schools, scouting groups, churches and youth organisations that want to partner with us.

Do I have to pay to get help?

No. Joining the community is free. Nobody has to donate, recruit others or pay a fee to receive support.

Is Bethel Iminators a treatment or counselling service?

No. We are a mentorship and community initiative, not a clinical service. We offer encouragement and practical resources and can point people towards qualified professionals. If gambling is seriously affecting your health, please speak to a trusted adult or a qualified professional.

How can a school, scouting group or youth organisation work with us?

Contact us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email and tell us about your group. We are open to talks, mentorship sessions and partnerships, and we will reply to discuss what is possible.

How is Bethel Iminators funded?

Through voluntary contributions from supporters, including a planned supporter plan of ₦100 a month or ₦1,200 a year, and a one-off appeal for a laptop. All gifts are voluntary.

Will I get start-up funding for a business?

Start-up funding is a long-term goal, not a promise. Any award will depend on funds raised, published eligibility rules, a documented merit-based assessment and legal review.

Terms and notices
Supporter plan terms

The supporter plan is a voluntary contribution of ₦100 a month or ₦1,200 a year. It is a gift, not a purchase, investment, loan or membership that buys any benefit, and it gives no priority for support or funding. It renews automatically on the same date until you cancel. Cancel any time using the manage-subscription link in your Paystack email, or email us your supporter code. Cancelling stops future charges but does not refund past ones, except where the law requires or in case of a payment error reported within 7 days. Contributions fund Bethel Iminators programme and running costs, and we will report income and spending publicly. Payments are processed by Paystack.

Laptop fund terms

The laptop fund is a one-off, personal fundraiser for equipment for our founder’s work on Bethel Iminators. Gifts are made by direct bank transfer to the founder’s account and are confirmed manually by us, which can take time. A receipt code is issued once we confirm that the money has arrived. Gifts are voluntary, are not purchases, investments or loans, carry no return or priority for support, and are not tax-deductible. Transfers are generally non-refundable except where the law requires or if you sent money in error and tell us within 7 days. Any amount above the cost of the laptop and delivery will be used for Bethel Iminators programme costs and reported publicly. The receipt acknowledges your gift. It is not a tax receipt.

Privacy notice (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023)

We collect your name, email and optional message to process your gift, issue your receipt and, only if you ask, contact you about the project. Your email is never shown publicly. Your first name appears on the supporters list unless you choose anonymity. Card details are handled by Paystack and never reach us. Data is stored with Supabase. To access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent, email us. Case information about gambling harm is shared only in anonymised form.

Important notices

Bethel Iminators is a community and mentorship initiative. It is not a bank, investment scheme, lottery, betting platform or clinical treatment service. Programmes and funding described here are goals, not guarantees. If gambling is harming you, please speak to a trusted person or a qualified health professional.
